Method
  1. Point forecast. Mean of three NWP models (ECMWF IFS, NOAA GFS, DWD ICON) via Open-Meteo, converted to AC power for your system: NOAA solar position at the interval midpoint, Erbs decomposition, isotropic-sky transposition, NOCT temperature derate, losses.
  2. Generic range. 19 quantiles from an error model in clear-sky-index space, per sky state, pooled over 16 reference cities on six continents: what a forecaster knows before it has seen your location.
  3. Reliability check. Past day-ahead forecasts at this location are scored against what happened: ERA5 reanalysis by default, or your own measured output if you import it. Coverage is always reported with interval width, with Wilson 95% intervals.
  4. Local recalibration. Each quantile is shifted separately per sky state (fixes bias, width and skew), recency-weighted, at a strength picked by a backtest on older weeks only. The newest weeks are used purely to judge it. No model retraining.
  5. Daily totals add hourly quantiles, assuming a cloudy day is cloudy all day. Truth from ERA5 is a reanalysis, not a sensor on your roof, which is why importing your own data matters.
